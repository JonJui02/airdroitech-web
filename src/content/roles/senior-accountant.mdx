## About the role

The purpose of this role is to provide leadership and supervision for the Malaysian Finance and Admin team, ensuring all HR matters are dealt with, and to offer support to Polyaire Australia in areas related to Finance, accounts payable, accounts receivable and financial reporting functions, etc.

## Primary duties and responsibilities

### Finance-related duties

- Update the landed cost calculation on a daily/weekly basis
- Assist the inventory team when/as required
- Perform month-end/year-end reporting
- Prepare all necessary journals and accruals as required
- Prepare and present regular financial reports to senior management and stakeholders
- Prepare monthly balance sheet reconciliations
- Ensure compliance with financial reporting standards and regulations
- Fixed assets maintenance and reconciliation (including updating the assets register)
- Manage and maintain the following ledgers:
  - Cash Book
  - Fixed Assets
  - General Ledger
  - Accounts Payable
  - Accounts Receivable
  - Inventory

### Business portfolios analysis

- Provides analysis and interpretation of technical business problems, identifies the root cause of these issues and presents solutions to help improve business processes and streamline operations
- Develops and maintains detailed documentation of business processes, policies, and procedures

### Customer sales order processing

- Ensure all sales orders are checked and processed correctly through the system
- Liaise with key stakeholders in Australia to resolve any issues with orders or specifications encountered at the processing stage
- Assist in addressing queries relating to order processing, sales credits, sales returns and invoicing to ensure timely payments from customers

### Accounts payable/receivable

- Assist in managing all activities related to the accounts payable function to ensure timely payments to vendors and maintain accurate records
- Assist in managing all activities related to the accounts receivable function, such as collection activities, bank reconciliations and sending follow-up enquiries related to overdue accounts
- Recommend improvements to accounts payable/receivable processes
- Generate reports to management as and when required

### General and people management

- Oversee the daily operations and general administration of the Finance and Admin team in Malaysia
- Ensure good customer service and improve the administration process
- Evaluate the performance and productivity of the team as/when required
- Implement and enforce policies and procedures

## Specific measures of success

- Maintain an effective and productive relationship with stakeholders
- Maintain an effective and professional functioning team
- Meet timelines for account management activities and achievement of Finance, Accounts Payable and Accounts Receivable targets
- Ensure the Finance and Admin team responds promptly to all key stakeholder projects and expectations

## Requirements and skills

- Bachelor's degree in commerce/accounting or equivalent
- CPA or CA qualified with a minimum of 5-10 years of industry experience
- Experience in high inventory movement, wholesale or FMCG with multi-source international suppliers
- Able to travel overseas when required
- Demonstrates good business acumen and analytical capabilities
- Strong stakeholder management skills to successfully manage a wide network of relationships
- Excellent interpersonal, customer service and negotiation skills
- Able to accurately articulate ideas, information, and presentations to a target audience
- Confident liaising with and updating clients, with sound written and verbal communication skills
- Exceptional attention to detail with numbers, and strong analytical skills for reports and data
- Advanced skills in the MS Office suite, i.e. Excel, Word, etc.
- Strong communication skills with a good command of English, both verbal and written
- Desirable: ERP knowledge, Epicor or similar

## Key attributes

- A strong work ethic
- Capable of identifying opportunities, mapping business process improvements and customer needs satisfaction
- Well organised and professional in driving quality customer experience and collection outcomes
- A natural drive to achieve results, combined with resilience and a continuous-improvement mindset
- A good communicator and negotiator
- Tenacity of purpose and dedication to achieving positive outcomes
